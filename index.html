About

Project manager with a communication and community management background and a strong strategic marketing foundation. I started by founding a student community and now study Intercultural Business Communication at EMUNI.

I founded and led GenC FSEGso, a student community for skill development and better campus life. I ran its social media strategy, organised workshops and managed the partnerships behind them.

Since 2022 I have worked on-site at Cuba In Tunisia, a week-long Cuban festival that runs around the clock, looking after international guests, artists and VIP attendees. I also manage social accounts as a freelance community manager and take part in European Solidarity Corps programmes, including the Learning from Nature exchange in Nefta and, most recently, the Y-BLOOM exchange in Sousse.
